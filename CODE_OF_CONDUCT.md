# CovScript Code of Conduct & Ethics

## Code of Conduct

The CovScript community welcomes everyone, regardless of experience level, age, gender, sexual orientation, race, religion, or physical ability.

We expect participants to:

- **Be respectful.** Diverse perspectives and backgrounds enrich our community. Disagreement is acceptable; personal attacks are not.
- **Be welcoming to newcomers.** Everyone's first line of code was once someone else's help. Answer questions with patience, point out mistakes with kindness.
- **Communicate constructively.** Critique code, not people. Explaining "why" is more valuable than saying "bad".
- **Respect privacy.** Do not disclose others' contact information or private details without permission.

Violations of this code may result in temporary or permanent removal from the community. If you experience or witness misconduct, please contact maintainers via GitHub Issues or email.

## Ethics

### Language as a Bridge

A programming language is more than a tool. It is a language between people, and between people and machines. A good language should let ideas flow without syntax getting in the way — accessible to beginners, precise for experts.

CovScript strives for simplicity without shallowness, power without bloat.

### Everything Has a Name

Behind every line of code lies a real world. The open-source community is sustained by countless individuals; the natural world around us deserves the same attention.

Each CovScript minor version (`x.y`) is named after an endangered or protected animal species — a reminder that technology should extend its goodwill to the world beyond the screen.

| Version | Name | Common Name | IUCN Status |

> In July 2022, the IUCN officially declared the Chinese Paddlefish (Psephurus gladius) extinct. It lived on Earth for approximately 150 million years, alongside the dinosaurs, yet vanished in our time. Version 3.2 bears its name — a memorial, and a warning.

### Open & Free

CovScript is open-source under the Apache 2.0 license — free to use, modify, and distribute. We believe openness is the foundation of technological progress, and community participation makes the language better.

### Steady Forward

CovScript commits to no breaking changes in this repository before major version `4.0` — all `4.0` features are currently developed and validated through [Extended CovScript](https://github.com/covscript/ecs). Every feature should be thoroughly tested and discussed. Stability matters more than speed.
